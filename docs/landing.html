Built for One More Run

Each world teaches one clear trick, then mixes it with a new food monster, a fresh piece of farm machinery, and a boss with a readable opening.

Short stages make it easy to try another kid, chase a cleaner score, or rescue the whole produce crew again.

Two at a timeOne boss per corner. Bosses stay in their corners. Only shots you can see coming cross the middle.
Fair fightsOnly one boss attacks at a time, so every dizzy opening is yours to mash.
Pop dropsEvery pop drops a pep pie and a power refill in the middle. Pick one: the other pops away.
One More TryOut of lives? Jump back in from your last pop. Popped bosses stay popped.

Only crowns you earn ever show up. No empty slots. Any kid can enter, all five crew powers start rescued, and quick kid switching is on, until you add spice.

Add spice

  • Solo Kid No kid switching.
  • No Crew No crew powers or power drops.
  • One Life One life per try.
  • No Second Wind Bosses never soften after a wipe.
  • Shuffle The middle bosses come in a new order.

Spices open after your first Royale clear and add 1,000 points each. Your best runs land on a local top five by score and by time, right on this device.

For Grown-Ups

The plain facts, cob by cob.

Short version: this is a silly cartoon platformer built from the start to be safe and kind. Here is exactly what it does and does not do.

🌐
Plays in a web browserNo download, no install, nothing to set up.
🚫
No ads, no purchasesNothing to buy, ever. No in-app anything.
🔒
No sign-in, no accountProgress, settings, and high scores stay in this browser. The game code sends no gameplay analytics.
🔌
No gameplay serversNo online leaderboard, chat, multiplayer lobby, or child profile. Scores use browser storage on this device.
🎈
Cartoon action onlyKids bonk cartoon food that pops and hops back up. No blood, no gore, nothing meant to frighten. The villains are goofy: a grumpy butter stick, squabbling shakers.
🎯
Made for ages 8 to 12Younger kids are welcome alongside a grown-up.
💛
Forgiving and kindPause anytime during play. A missed jump is never the kid's fault; the game just says “One More Try.”
🎮
Keyboard, gamepad, or touchControls are simple. Keyboard keys can be remapped.
🌙
Motion and contrast controlsReduce motion, high contrast, larger text, and visual sound cues are available in Settings.
🌻
AI-assisted assets disclosedSelected art and audio used generative tools, then received human direction, selection, editing, and review. The game uses no runtime AI.

The design rule behind every villain in the game: cute-menace, never cruel.

Hosting services may keep ordinary request logs. KILLER CORN itself includes no ads, analytics, or trackers.
